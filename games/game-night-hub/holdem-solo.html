Hold'em

How to play

You get two cards only you can see. Five shared cards come out in the middle: three (the flop), then one (the turn), then one more (the river). Your best five-card hand from all seven wins. Bet when you like your chances, fold when you don't.

On your turn: Fold (give up this hand), Check (pass if nobody has bet), Call (match the bet), or Raise (bet more). Keys: F, C, R.
