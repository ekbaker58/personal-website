        How to play
        • Tap, click, or press Space, W or ↑ to flap. Fly through the gaps.
        • 1 coin per pipe. Medals at 10, 20, 30 and 40 pay bonus coins.
        • Spend coins on skins. Some skins can only be earned.
        • The sky changes at 25, 50 and 100.
        • Coins float in some gaps (2 each), and scraping past a pipe is a close call (+1).
        • P or Esc pauses a solo flight.
        At game night
        • ☀️ Daily: the same pipes for everyone all day, with today's leader flying beside you. The day's winner gets 50 coins.
        • When the host starts a race, everyone flies the same pipes at once.
        • Tap anyone's name to duel them, poke them, whisper, gift coins, prank them mid-flight or put a bounty on them.
        • Chat commands: /w /me /roll /gift /poke /duel /prank /bounty /stats /who /daily. Type /help in the chat.
        • / or T opens the chat on a computer.

        Playing solo: scores and coins stay on this device. Races, duels and chat are for game night.
